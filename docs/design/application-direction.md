# Application redesign

Mode: Operate. Scope: the shared shell and all existing application screens.

## Direction contract

THESIS: ordered working files. A service desk should feel like a well-indexed collection of active work, with a stable navigation spine, quiet surfaces and precise actions.

OWN-WORLD: light cool paper, dark blue ink, Noto Sans Hebrew, outlined panels, compact rounded controls. Blue marks action and selection; semantic feedback retains its established roles. No decorative gradients or hovering cards.

STORY: requesters follow cases, agents scan and act, implementers configure. Preserve every existing action and source of business values. Show each page's title, purpose and primary action immediately.

FIRST VIEWPORT: a 248px navigation column at the reading edge, a compact utility header, and a broad reading surface. The portal uses an inline summary and a continuous case list; the same headers, controls and surfaces carry reports, details and settings. Mobile uses a menu drawer, stacked content and reachable primary actions.

FORM: grounded candidate 1, the indexed working-file system, selected explicitly by the user over candidate 7 (operations console). Seed 1a9c78d3. Other grounded candidates: service directory, dispatch ledger, route map, document register, laboratory worksheet. The six external challengers were declined for daily-task clarity and audience fit; retain disciplined grouping from the cutting bench, traceable navigation from HyperCard, press feedback from the console, readable emphasis from captions, clear discrete states from folding, and restrained contrast hierarchy from stage lighting.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: press feedback on pointer actions and short ease-out drawer/dialog transitions; no entrance choreography for repeated work. Reduced-motion preference removes nonessential motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
